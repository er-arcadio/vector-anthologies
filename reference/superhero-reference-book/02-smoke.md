## 2. Smoke

**Identity:** Blasian woman. Trained as a ninja — raised *inside* the camp/order that trained her rather than seeking it out (Batman-adjacent origin, distinct execution). Slender, curly hair worn down in civilian life. Skin tone/nationality intentionally left ambiguous.

**Visual duality signal:** Pulling her hair into a ponytail (skunk-tail echo) is the explicit cue that she's about to suit up.

**Power:** No superpowers — mastery of hand-to-hand combat, parkour, and acrobatics. Signature tools: small smoke machines; scent/chemical takedowns (unconsciousness, laughing gas, perfumes); wall-sticking ninja tools; spiked knuckles (painful, non-lethal). Belt carries pill-form smoke/scent canisters. Deliberately stays lightweight and agile rather than escalating toward Iron Man/Batman-style heavy tech.

**Tragedy:** Killed without hesitation as part of her training, until a specific moment made her realize the people she was killing didn't deserve it — people can change and deserve the chance to prove it. That realization is her ongoing moral code. *(Open: needs one specific triggering incident, not a gradual realization.)*

**Present life:** Goes to therapy, has a real supportive friend group — actively healed, not perpetually brooding. Prefers not to kill.

**Key differentiator:** Inverts the usual formula — for Smoke, ordinary civilian life is where she finds joy (bubbly, jubilant, lighthearted); the hero identity is the necessary weight she carries, not the escape. This is what most clearly separates her from Black Widow.

**Dual life:** Bubbly, joyful civilian by day; serious, precise vigilante by night.

**Open:** The specific incident that flipped her moral code.
