## 7. Chicle

**Identity:** Afro-Spanish man. Nicknamed "Chicle" (Spanish for chewing gum) by his parents as a kid for constantly chewing gum — a warm, specific, human origin for the name.

**Power:** Extremely durable, taut, stretchy skin — nearly impossible to cut or pierce in normal combat (avoids classic rubber weaknesses like tearing/melting). Can self-amputate a limb (octopus-style autotomy) as a weapon or escape option, then regrow it — framed as a genuinely difficult, rare, last-resort act that visibly costs him, never a casual tool.

> 🔓 **Broken-tier fit:** REGENERATION (BIOLOGY — self-only domain). Limb regrowth plus a durable, self-restructuring body puts him squarely on this branch — its broken tier is "near-perfect self-restoration." Worth noting the same domain rule applies here as with Chameleon: this stays self-only, regrowing what's his, not manufacturing new external matter.

**Power origin:** Experimented on by the government with a substance described as "a permanent botox that gives infinite life" — the biological source of his regenerative ability and durable skin. *(Open: confirmed as regeneration rather than true immortality/agelessness, pending final sign-off — affects Path A/B calibration.)*

**Tragedy:** Lost his army brothers — fellow soldiers, not a literal sibling — during the war. Presumed dead, but never confirmed. That uncertainty is a deliberate open thread, fueling both grief and an unresolved need to know what really happened (possible future hook: they weren't actually dead, echoing his own experimentation origin). Root of his personal, explicitly anti-war mission.

**Dual life:** Works for the government by day — the same institution that experimented on him, a sharp irony worth leaning into. Off-duty, operates as a vigilante who blends into homeless communities, who are routinely overlooked — giving him real cover and a genuine connection to the population he protects. Should be written with care and specificity, not as a disguise gimmick.

**Scope:** His government job deploys him globally, wherever conflict is active. His hands-on vigilante work stays grounded and local within whatever city he's in at the time — global reach through the job, street-level heroism through the persona.

**Open:**
- Confirm regeneration vs. true immortality
- Does the government know about his vigilante identity, or is it fully secret from his employer?
- Tone beyond "dark and gory" — fully brooding, or dark-with-humor?
