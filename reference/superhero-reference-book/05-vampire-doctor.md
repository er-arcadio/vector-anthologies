## 5. Vampire Doctor

**Identity:** Brunette woman, striking, heavy nightlife aesthetic with tattoos. Lives somewhere with permanent night.

**Power:** Vampirism — access to blood via her medical career gives her a legitimate, controlled supply. Power tier (mortal/fragile vs. near-immortal) still undecided.

> 🔓 **Potential broken-tier fit:** REGENERATION (BIOLOGY — self-only domain) — contingent on the Path A/B decision below. Only relevant if she lands on Path B (near-immortal); a fragile, mortal Path A version wouldn't reach this tier at all.

**Dual life:** Doctor with sanctioned blood access by day vs. predator by night — the strongest dual-life contrast in the roster as currently written, needing little extra work.

**Open (largest gaps in the roster):**
