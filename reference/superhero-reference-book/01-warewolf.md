## 1. Warewolf

**Identity:** Black man, scientist. Mostly human-looking but visibly changed — yellow eyes, build shifts from wiry/scrawny toward lumberjack-hairy-and-beefed, sharp nails, heightened animal senses (especially smell). Goes shirtless in transformed state. No disguise — his changed body composition already makes him hard to recognize.

**Power:** A serum he chooses to take — not an involuntary reaction like the Hulk. Grants super strength, near-indestructible durability, instinct-driven healing, high jumps, sharp nails, heightened smell, spidey-sense-like awareness. Ruthless in this state, but no claws.

> 🔓 **Potential broken-tier fit:** REGENERATION (BIOLOGY — self-only domain). His instinct-driven healing is only enhanced, not molecular-level, so this is a *potential* fit, not a confirmed one — worth revisiting if the serum's healing ever gets pushed toward the "molecular self-reconstruction" tier.

**Tragedy:** His family died because people chose violence. As a direct result, he's desperate to avoid violence — and specifically the serum — himself. Every fight is structured as his attempt to solve the problem *without* transforming, using his own science/inventions first. Taking the serum is always a last resort, visibly costly, often accompanied by tears or emotional numbness/blackout.

**Key mechanic — permanent cost:** Each use permanently converts ~1% of his human DNA toward the animal state. Progression is visible and sequential: more body hair → denser/stronger build → heightened senses → occasional "foggy" cognition. Real stakes for every use, with an implicit endpoint (what happens at 100%?) as a long-term story engine.

**Threat calibration:** Near-indestructible one-on-one, but vulnerable to **numbers** — he can't protect everyone in a group at once, reinforcing his loneliness/pack theme.

**Dual life:** Quiet scientist/inventor by day; reluctant, tormented berserker by night — a life built around *avoiding* his own power.

**Open:** Define his non-serum toolkit (gadgets/inventions), since that's his primary problem-solving mode for most of any fight.
