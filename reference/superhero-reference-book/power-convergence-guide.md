# Power Convergence Reference Guide

*A companion framework for calibrating any power in the roster against its theoretical ceiling. Each fundamental power tends toward one of a small number of "fundamental domains" as it scales from C (emerging) to BROKEN (fundamental). Use this to sanity-check how far any hero's power could go, and what it would look like if pushed to the edge.*

## How to read this

- **Tiers are strength bands, not equal steps.** A power that starts naturally strong (teleportation, telekinesis) sits farther right than one that starts weak (super speed) even at the same nominal tier.
- **BROKEN is a ceiling, not a starting assumption.** A power only earns a BROKEN-tier convergence if pushing it to its logical extreme actually lands somewhere coherent. If it doesn't, it's better left without one (see Size/Scale below).
- **Convergence is allowed.** Two branches that become functionally equivalent at their ceiling can merge into the same fundamental domain.
- **Self vs. external scope matters.** Powers that only ever act on the user's own body (regeneration, shapeshifting) stay in a self-only lane even when the underlying mechanism (molecular control) looks similar to a power that acts on the external world (telekinesis).

## Domain: MOTION → SPACETIME

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Super speed | Run fast | Combat reflexes | Relativistic velocity | Severe time dilation | Own clock approaches a stop — subjective time-stop / apparent teleportation, **not** solo backward causation |
| Flight | Hovering | Fast aerial | Supersonic | Near-relativistic | Rides super speed's ceiling — no separate convergence |

*Note on super speed's ceiling: a real FTL-causality paradox (the "tachyonic anti-telephone") does exist in relativity, but it requires two separate observers in different reference frames closing a loop — it does not support a single traveler revisiting and altering their own past. That's a Back to the Future-style premise fiction imports on top of relativity, not something the physics itself produces.*

## Domain: SPACETIME

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teleportation | Short jumps | Long range | Arbitrary + objects | Interplanetary | Dimensional transport |
| Portal creation | Local portal | Persistent portal | Battlefield portals | Interplanetary gateways | Dimensional geometry control |
| Gravity manipulation | Weight changes | Local gravity | Crushing fields | Planetary control | Extreme spacetime curvature |
| ↳ Spatial-size scaling *(folded in, not standalone)* | Occupy less/more space, mass unchanged | Giant/tiny w/ normal weight | Compress space around held objects | Compress large regions, no mass involved | Converges directly onto gravity's own ceiling — not a distinct fundamental domain |
| Time manipulation | Brief slow/rewind | Localized loops | Regional stasis | Large-scale rewind | Full temporal control |
| Precognition | Brief flashes | Short-term prediction | Branching futures | Near-complete future awareness | Perception across time itself — not reality-rewriting; acting on foreknowledge needs no powers, only the perception itself scales |

## Domain: MATTER

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Telekinesis | Small objects | People/vehicles | Multi-object precision | Molecular restructuring | Subatomic control of *external* matter — rearranges, never creates |
| Density manipulation | Small density shift | Dense/light body | Extreme compression | Near-intangibility | Fundamental matter-interaction states |
| Size/Scale — density version | Shrink/grow a few inches, mass conserved | Halve/double size | Insect- to building-scale | Approaching degenerate-matter density when shrunk | Total command of own compression, up to and including triggering own Schwarzschild collapse — a real physical threshold |

*Size/Scale, resolved: the density version (mass conserved, volume changes) earns its own row — its ceiling is a genuine physical threshold, not invented. The spatial version (mass decoupled from size) doesn't earn a standalone row; taken to its ceiling it becomes mass-independent geometry control, which is gravity manipulation's own endpoint with the mass requirement removed. Proportional scaling (mass and size scale together) is dropped entirely — it never approaches a fundamental-domain convergence, its actual ceiling is the square-cube law, a structural/engineering wall, not a broken tier.*

## Domain: BIOLOGY (self-only)

*Kept separate from MATTER: telekinesis at its ceiling can rearrange any matter in the world; these powers, however extreme, only ever restructure the user's own body.*

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regeneration | Rapid healing | Organ restoration | Cellular reconstruction | Molecular self-reconstruction | Near-perfect self-restoration (self, not external creation) |
| Shapeshifting | Appearance | Anatomy | Cellular adaptation | Molecular self-restructuring | Total biological self-design (self, not external creation) |

## Domain: ENERGY

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electromagnetism | Small effects | Powerful E/M | Fields, currents | Light + radiation | Fundamental EM control |
| Energy manipulation | Absorb/redirect | Large transfers | Transform energy forms | Massive radiation/thermal control | Extreme existing-energy conversion |

## Domain: MIND / INFORMATION

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Telepathy | Read thoughts | Deep mind reading | Memory/emotion editing | Large-scale mental control | Total cognitive control |
| Illusion / perception | Minor sensory tricks | Convincing illusions | Group-scale illusions | Undetectable mass illusion | Total perceptual control |

## Domain: REALITY

| Power | C | B | A | S | BROKEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Probability manipulation | Minor luck | Bias outcomes | Select among likely outcomes | Select among broad possibilities | Rewrite possibility itself |
| Causality manipulation | Alter small chains | Prevent consequences | Rewrite causal links | Break prerequisites | Rewrite why events happen — no clean isolated hero example found |
| Reality manipulation | Minor alteration | Broad alteration | Large-scale rewriting | Laws/possibilities can change | Creation/destruction + rule rewriting |

## Optional add-on: a density-battery build, worked example

*Applicable if any hero (e.g. Ghost, or a future density/size character) ever needs a size-shifting power calibrated against real-world materials rather than arbitrary "very strong" flavor text.*

**Mechanism:** rest at "normal" human density (1010 kg/m³) at a large giant size; shrinking down to combat size concentrates the same mass into a smaller frame, spiking density. Two worked builds, both resting at 8 ft, calibrated against different material thresholds:

| Person | Max height (normal density) | Density @ 8 ft | Density @ 7 ft | Density @ 6 ft | Density @ 5 ft | Black-hole height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Concrete-tier (30 MPa @ 8ft, 205.8 t) | 86 ft (26.2 m) | 1,257,491 kg/m³ — right at concrete's own limit; asphalt cracks underfoot | 1,877,071 kg/m³ — exceeds concrete's strength; every step cracks floors | 2,980,719 kg/m³ — ~2x concrete; floors regularly give way | 5,150,682 kg/m³ — ~3x concrete; steel/granite still hold | 2.2×10⁻²¹ m (smaller than a proton — physically unreachable, math only) |
| Steel-tier (250 MPa @ 8ft, 1,714.8 t) | 174 ft (53.2 m) | 10,479,066 kg/m³ — matches steel's yield and granite's crushing strength | 15,642,220 kg/m³ — exceeds steel's yield; beams buckle | 24,839,267 kg/m³ — ~2x steel; punches through steel/granite | 42,922,253 kg/m³ — ~3x steel, high-alloy territory; stainless gives way like cardboard | 1.8×10⁻²⁰ m (same order below a proton — physically unreachable, math only) |

*Honest caveats: at these masses (hundreds to a couple thousand tonnes), self-gravity stays undetectable (~0.0001 m/s² at arm's reach) — no "walking planet" effect at this scale, that only shows up at much larger (millions-of-tonnes) masses. The black-hole column is a mathematical curiosity, not a reachable story beat — the real density ceiling for a character like this is a narrative choice, not a physics wall.*
