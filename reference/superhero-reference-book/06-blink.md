## 6. Blink

**Identity:** Light-skinned brunette woman. Carries a black baton that extends into a staff. All-royal-blue suit with a mask, Robin-esque silhouette — **color possibly switching to orange** to pair with the hummingbird totem (open decision). Tone stays goofy/light.

**Totem:** Hummingbird — locked. Strong fit for teleportation: blink-fast repositioning mirrors a hummingbird's near-invisible burst movement and sudden stop-start motion. Hummingbirds are also fiercely territorial despite their size, suiting a "goofy but don't underestimate her" character.

**Power:** Teleportation, self and others.

> 🔓 **Broken-tier fit:** TELEPORTATION (SPACETIME domain). This is the cleanest match in the whole roster — teleportation's own broken tier is literally "dimensional/spacetime transport." Useful reference if her power limits (below) ever get pushed toward planetary/interplanetary range.

**Power limits — in progress:** A 1-second cooldown between jumps was proposed as the sole limitation, but on its own (no range cap, no line-of-sight requirement) it likely makes her too strong — near-total evasion and repeated instant-rescue with no real downside, undercutting Criterion 1. Recommended fix: pair the cooldown with either a **range cap per jump** (line-of-sight or a set radius) or a **"must have been there before"** rule for long-distance jumps, while leaving short local hops unrestricted. Not yet finalized.

**Tragedy:** She once could only save one of two people and had to choose who to grab and who to leave. Concept locked; the specific incident (who, when, what happened to the one left behind) still needs defining. Recommendation: every team-teleport in a fight should echo this choice — who she grabs first, who's left exposed for a beat — making the power itself a recurring emotional trigger.
