## 3. Chameleon

**Identity:** Black man, goofy tone. Keeps a pet chameleon as a grounded, literal metaphor for his own identity — part of why the codename stays even though his power isn't a literal biological match.

**Power:** Touch-transformation — becomes what he touches. True default is human form; preferred *combat* state is concrete, since he fights primarily in the city and it's everywhere around him — a grounded, urban choice reinforcing his tie to the human world.

> 🔓 **Broken-tier fit:** SHAPESHIFTING (BIOLOGY — self-only domain). Touch-transformation is a clean match for this branch's ceiling — molecular self-restructuring, total biological self-design. Note this stays self-only: he's remaking *his own* composition to mimic what he touches, not rearranging external matter, so it doesn't cross into MATTER/Telekinesis territory.

**Tragedy:** Parents died in a fire. As a direct result, he has a real, recurring fear/avoidance of fire specifically — a concrete weakness that can resurface in fights, not a generic Achilles' heel.

**Status:** Concept is close to locked — concrete-as-default resolved an earlier mechanical overlap with Ghost's power set.

**Open:** None currently blocking.
