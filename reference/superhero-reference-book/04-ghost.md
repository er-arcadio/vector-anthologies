## 4. Ghost

**Identity:** Tan brunette in human form; transforms into a pale, silver-blonde, almost corpse-like state as Ghost. Wears an eye mask (Robin-style) even in ghost form as a practical safeguard. All-white suit, black eyes. No flight.

**Power:** Phasing (self and others). Heals while in ghost form — offset directly by the cost below.

> 🔓 **Broken-tier fit:** DENSITY MANIPULATION (MATTER domain). Phasing is functionally the extreme end of this branch's ceiling — "near-intangibility / extreme mass-density states." Worth keeping in mind if her power ever needs a hard limit: the density table's broken tier is bounded by real matter-interaction rules, not unlimited pass-through.

**Key mechanic — reverse memory loss:** Every hour spent in ghost form costs her one hour of memory. A direct, literal, ticking-clock cost tied to the power itself — the longer she stays phased, the more she loses touch with reality and her own past. Makes "how long do I stay transformed" a real tactical and emotional decision every fight.

**Tragedy:** Sole survivor of an accident that killed her family. Her powers originate from her own near-death experience in that same event. She resents having been the one who survived.

**Emotional state:** Genuinely afraid of her own power and of the ghost state itself. Currently a combat novice, relying on other tactics, but hopes to grow into using the power itself as her strength over time — built-in room for a growth arc.

**Totem:** Resolved as intentionally animal-free — a relatable archetype (ghost/spirit) works fine on its own.

**Open:** What her novice fighting style looks like now vs. what she's working toward.
