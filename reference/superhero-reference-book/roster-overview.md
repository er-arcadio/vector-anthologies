# Superhero Reference Book

*Source of truth for the roster — supersedes the original brainstorm list. Update this doc as concepts evolve.*

## Core Criteria

1. **Power matched by world** — Path A (grounded/human-scale) or Path B (near-godlike, matched by an equally scaled threat). Either way, something must be able to genuinely hurt them — physically, emotionally, or morally.
2. **Identity-defining tragedy, present in every fight** — not just origin-story backstory; it has to visibly resurface in the mechanics/choreography of every confrontation.
3. **Full animal integration** — the totem shows up in how they move, fight, and think, using real behavior — not just a name or costume motif.
4. **Committed tone** — fully light/fun or fully dark/brooding. No weak middle ground.
5. **Two aspirational, contrasting identities** — both halves interesting/enviable in their own way, sharp contrast between them.
6. **Visual/physical signal of duality** — the shift between identities (or control/loss of control) should be visible in real time.
7. **Grounded, human world** on at least one side of the dual life.
8. **Strength rooted in skill, resilience, or cost** — not just raw power, even in Path B characters.
