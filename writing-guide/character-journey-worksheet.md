# Character Journey Worksheet

A template. Fill one out per POV/major character **before** outlining events for a new arc or story — see `outline-checklist.md`, which references this as its Section 0 and checks individual beats against it from Section 2 onward.

---

**Character:** ___________

- [ ] **Want** (stated goal, what they're chasing): ___________
- [ ] **Need** (what they actually have to learn/accept to grow): ___________
- [ ] **The gap** — one line on how want and need conflict, or will eventually: ___________
- [ ] **Starting state** — who they are, what they believe, at beat one: ___________
- [ ] **Ending state** — who they are, what they believe, at the close: ___________
- [ ] **Stakes if they fail the want:** ___________
- [ ] **Cost if they succeed at the want:** ___________ (what does winning take from them?)
- [ ] **Cost if they get the need instead:** ___________ (what do they have to give up on the want?)

**Strengths → obstacle type they're naturally equipped for:**
- [ ] Strength 1: ___________ → handles which obstacle type well (external/interpersonal/internal)?

**Weaknesses → obstacle type the plot should specifically target:**
- [ ] Weakness 1: ___________ → which obstacle type should exploit this?
- [ ] Weakness 2 (optional): ___________ → which obstacle type should exploit this?

- [ ] **The weakness is load-bearing** — at least one major event in the sequence should fail (or nearly fail) specifically *because* of this weakness, not bad luck.
- [ ] **The strength has a shadow side** — note where over-relying on the strength backfires or blinds them to something. A strength never tested for its downside stays flat.

**Relationship check (for multi-POV or ensemble stories):**
- [ ] Where does this character's want put them in friction with another character's want?
- [ ] Where is their want in synergy with another's — and is that synergy tested at least once rather than staying comfortable throughout?
