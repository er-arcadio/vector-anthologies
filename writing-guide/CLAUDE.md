# CLAUDE.md — writing-guide/

Three files. Read `style-guide.md` and `story-laws.md` before drafting anything in `stories/`. `fiction-style-profile.md` is the same kind of preference rules, just kept in its original research form rather than rewritten as drafting-ready instructions — read `style-guide.md` while actually writing, and go to the profile for the reasoning behind a rule or to extend it with a new book comparison.

- `style-guide.md` — tone, voice, prose technique. Preferences, but firm ones. This is the drafting-ready version to follow — it already incorporates the sustained-dialogue and mostly-present-tense defaults from `fiction-style-profile.md`.
- `story-laws.md` — hard canon constraints. Not preferences. If a story idea conflicts with one of these, flag it rather than resolve it silently.
- `fiction-style-profile.md` — the reader-preference research (real books ranked against each other) that `style-guide.md`'s prose defaults were derived from — still real preference rules, just not yet in imperative, drafting-ready form. If it and `style-guide.md` ever seem to disagree, that means the profile hasn't been fully folded in yet — fix `style-guide.md` rather than splitting the difference while drafting.

If any of these files needs to change because canon or preference has evolved, update it directly — but treat that as a deliberate decision, not an incidental edit while drafting a story.
