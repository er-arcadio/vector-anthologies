# Story Laws

Hard constraints. Unlike the style guide, these are not preferences — violating one is a canon break. If a story idea requires breaking one of these, stop and flag it for a decision rather than quietly deciding it yourself.

## Power system

- **Vector's power is knowledge-dependent and this rule never breaks.** His ceiling scales with his comprehension of reality. He can only control what he comprehends. No exceptions, no convenient one-off power-ups that bypass this.
- **The playing-god ceiling is a standing refusal, not a limitation.** Eli is physically capable of crossing it (molecular-level reality rewriting) and chooses not to, daily. Never write this as something he *can't* do — it must always read as something he won't.
- **"Ethics and physics are the same thing"** — his precision of understanding is what keeps his most destructive abilities surgical rather than catastrophic. Don't let a story imply sloppy or careless use of his top-tier abilities; imprecision would contradict the character's core discipline.
- **Vinny's self-healing is an integration mechanism, not passive defense.** He absorbs and optimizes against damage, foreign power, and dark influence. By Arc 7 he is structurally the most powerful being in the story. Any story touching Vinny should not accidentally write him as merely defensive or reactive.

## Villain roster

- The core villain roster is fixed to Group 2 classmates from the Liberty Science Center field trip, with the exception of Brick. Every one of their fights is, underneath, the same question: what did the accident — and everything before it — make you? A new villain for a short story should either honor this origin logic or be clearly framed as outside the original cohort (e.g., unconnected to the accident entirely) rather than quietly breaking the pattern.

## Ambiguous threads — do not resolve

Some threads were deliberately left open in the source material. Do not confirm, explain, or close these unless a specific future decision says otherwise:

- The origin of Vinny's Ouroboros ring (never confirmed — implication only).
- The devil's endgame/identity (kept purely atmospheric — never face-to-face).
- Vinny's whereabouts and fate after his Arc 7 disappearance.

## Character consistency

- **Vinny's psychological register is "Dexter baseline," not Joker.** His empathy starts genuinely high and declines through specific, trackable losses and choices — his intelligence never degrades, only the weight he gives to others' experience of his actions. He gets exactly one "Joker crack" allowed in the whole story (a laugh that arrives wrong). Do not write him as unhinged or theatrical outside that single beat.
- **Eli's growth is "victory over self," not victory over enemies.** Any new story should be checked against this: does the climax turn on Eli out-fighting someone, or on Eli choosing something harder than winning? The latter is the story's actual engine.
- **The suit reflects Eli's relationship to vulnerability at that point in time**, not just fashion/tech progression (absurd → earnest → impressive → iconic → simplified → symbolic). If writing a story set at a specific arc, check `story-bible/suit-evolution.md` for what he'd actually be wearing then.

## Timeline discipline

- Check `story-bible/plot-outline/` for the arc a new story is set in, and `story-bible/foreshadowing-map.md` + `story-bible/plot-twist-inventory.md` before writing — a short story set inside an existing arc must not contradict a seed or payoff already placed there.
- Character ages, power stages (Think/Know/Feel-Become — see `story-bible/power-system/power-progression.md`), and relationship status are all arc-locked. Don't borrow a later-arc capability or relationship beat into an earlier-set story.

## Reference roster boundary

`reference/superhero-reference-book/` is a separate roster for future anthology entries and is **not** currently connected to Vector continuity. Do not cross characters over between the two without an explicit decision to do so.
