# Eli's Character Arc

 *"A boy who learned to control the world before he learned to inhabit it."*

**Phase 1 --- Containment (Arc 1, Age 16)**

-   **Wound:** Father's voice inside him. Nothing ever enough. Powers are another thing to perfect and another way to fail.

-   **Mechanism:** Control through knowledge. Master it before anyone can use it against him.

-   **Defining moment:** Cleaning his room before parents wake. Not marveling --- managing the evidence.

**Phase 2 --- Justification (Arcs 2-3, Ages 17.5-21)**

-   **Wound:** Power outpacing judgment. Fighting evil partly because it's right and partly because it's the one thing his father would approve of without reservation.

-   **Mechanism:** Intellectual framing. Every moral question becomes a physics problem.

-   **Defining moment:** The citywide blackout --- treats human consequences as a technical problem.

**Phase 3 --- Fracture (Arcs 4-5, Age 22)**

-   **Wound:** Killed someone he knew. Correct decision. Doesn't feel the way correct decisions are supposed to feel.

-   **Mechanism:** Compartmentalization. Surgical residency as both processing and avoidance simultaneously.

-   **Defining moment:** Father's dinner table verdict on Vector's killing. Eli cannot say a word.

**Phase 4 --- Dissolution (Arc 6, Age 23-26)**

-   **Wound:** Everything arrives at once. Mother killed. Identity exposed and re-concealed. Approaching omnipotence and feeling emptier than expected.

-   **The apathy:** The hunger that drove him finds less to grab onto. What is all of this for?

-   **Mother's death:** Loses the only person who knew both versions of him and loved them without contradiction.

-   **The retreat:** Goes because he ran. Stays because leaving requires knowing where to go. Colombia. Four to six months.

-   **Defining moment:** First successful meditation --- feels the field as presence not data. He has been inside his own head his entire life.

**Phase 5 --- Integration (Arc 7, Age 27)**

-   **What's different:** Not controlling the world --- participating in it. Fighting evil because it's right, not because anyone is watching.

-   **Father resolution:** Tells his father who he is and what he's done. Doesn't require a particular response. The most grown-up thing he does in the entire story.

-   **Defining moment:** Vinny disappearing mid-fight. Score unresolved. Standing in the aftermath --- not reaching for control, not filing it away. Just present. Letting it be unfinished.

**Containment → Justification → Fracture → Dissolution → Integration**

*Hide it → Explain it → Break under it → Lose it → Become it*
