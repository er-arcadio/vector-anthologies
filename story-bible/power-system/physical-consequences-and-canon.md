# Physical Consequences & Scientific Canon

-   **Stage 3:** No meaningful cost. Effortless. Like walking.

-   **Stage 2 overuse:** Cognitive and physical fatigue. Mental fog, physical heaviness, glucose crash equivalent. **Stage 2 costs him after.**

-   **Stage 1 overuse:** Immediate neurological disruption. Tinnitus, spatial disorientation, field perception stuttering. Cannot be pushed through. **Stage 1 costs him during.**

-   **Cumulative effects:** Chronic overuse produces persistent low-grade cognitive fog. The meditation retreat in Arc 6 is partly about recovering from years of cumulative overuse he never properly addressed.

-   **Sensory overload:** In chaotic environments the volume of simultaneous field sensing becomes genuinely overwhelming. His strength becomes a liability when the environment is too complex.

-   **Morality as active constraint:** There are moments where Eli could end a confrontation immediately by doing something he won't do. This costs him fights. The audience feels it as tension not just theme.

**Subatomic Deconstruction --- Energy Ceiling (Scientific Canon)**

Eli is not converting mass to energy. He is disrupting coherence ---
severing electromagnetic bonds that hold matter in organized form
without triggering nuclear release. Matter loses its arrangement, not
its existence. Particle fog: the atoms are still there, drifting, no
longer organized into the pattern that made them a person or an object.

Energy released is bond energy only --- electromagnetic and molecular,
not nuclear. Still substantial at scale (hence EM pulse and heat) but
orders of magnitude below E=mc² conversion. The nuclear threshold
exists. Eli knows exactly where it is. The playing god ceiling is partly
the knowledge that he could cross it. His precision of understanding is
what makes the ability surgical rather than catastrophic --- his ethics
and his physics are the same thing.

**Blood transfer mechanism:** Vinny's cellular byproducts are foreign
material to a body without the same regenerative infrastructure. The
cult leader's immune system treated it as catastrophic invasion ---
accelerated organ failure. This is why the Shockwave transfer works
differently: Vinny transferred Brick's acquired power, not his own
biology. He learned from the cult leader's death what not to transfer.

**Field Sensing --- Neurological Reality (Scientific Canon)**

The nervous system has no existing mechanism for filtering constant
full-spectrum electromagnetic perception. Learning to manage it should
feel like the body literally building new circuitry --- painful,
disorienting, slow. The Colombia retreat is where this building occurs.

-   **Weeks 1-4 (Breaking open):** The retreat's electromagnetic quiet doesn't feel like relief --- it feels like withdrawal. Field perception reaches further without permission. Colombia's biological density (among the highest on earth --- more species per square meter than almost any other environment; natural complexity has pattern where urban complexity has noise) means the field is extraordinarily rich. Headaches, hypersensitivity, sleep that doesn't rest. This is when the first hookup with Iris happens.

-   **Weeks 5-12 (Building):** The brain finds patterns without conscious decision. Individual signatures become differentiable. Differentiation is the foundation of filtering. Headaches become intermittent. The period of drift into friendship with Iris.

-   **Weeks 13-24 (Plateau):** Filtering possible but costly. Works in the retreat's clean environment. No idea if it holds under urban complexity. Iris arrives during this period. The second hookup, the real conversation, the field trip revelation.

**Return to New York:** He left believing his identity was public
knowledge. He returns to find people don't recognize him --- Maya's
erasure held. Then the city's electromagnetic field hits him like a
physical impact. Every phone, every power line, every body
simultaneously. Thirty seconds of almost-failure. He stops trying to
filter and receives. The field stabilizes. He gets in a cab. Iris lands
an hour later. Takes her own cab. They don't coordinate.
