# Arc-by-Arc Power Progression

-   **Arc 0 (Age 11):** None. The accident happens and nothing manifests — for Eli or, visibly, for anyone. Onset is staggered per person across the following years (Brick early, Eli late); the mechanism behind that variance isn't settled, see `../plot-outline/arc-00.md`.

-   **Arc 1 (Age 16):** Stage 1 only. Push/pull, short lifts, crude flight, crude shields, environmental disruption. Everything costs him *during* use (see `physical-consequences-and-canon.md`) — nothing is smooth this arc.

-   **Arc 2 (Age 17.5-18):** Stage 2: push/pull, short lifts. Stage 1: force shields refined, heavy lifts, proximity sensing, force redirection, subatomic awareness.

-   **Arc 3 (Age 19-21):** Stage 3: push/pull, short lifts. Stage 2: heavy lifts, force shields, proximity sensing. Stage 1: subatomic deconstruction, molecular state change, element bending, electricity, motion freeze.

-   **Arc 4 (Age 22):** Stage 3: heavy lifts, force shields. Stage 2: subatomic deconstruction, element bending, molecular state change. Stage 1: spatial manipulation, matter recognition, targeted motion freeze.

-   **Arc 5 (Graduation):** Stage 3: subatomic deconstruction. Stage 2: motion freeze, molecular state change, electricity. Stage 1: matter creation, deep matter recognition, novel substance invention.

-   **Arc 6 (Age 23-26):** Stage 3: motion freeze, electricity, molecular state change. Stage 2: matter creation, field sensing. Stage 1: field sensing, self-healing, body system control, psychic beam detection, electromagnetic neural shielding.

-   **Arc 7 (Age 27):** Stage 3: field sensing, self-healing, body system control. Stage 2: matter creation. Stage 1: gravitational field mastery where time dilation becomes a measurable consequence.
