# Shockwave — Force/Destruction + Transferred Super Strength

-   **Arcs:** 2 (contained), 5 (transformed, killed)

-   **Tragedy:** Tried to get help before becoming a villain. Doctor didn't believe him. Government hotline didn't exist. Failed by the system before Eli.

-   **Arc 5:** Yellow Hulk-like transformation. Constant pain. Involuntary discharges. Civilian casualties mounting. Every containment attempt failing. The "not instantaneous" is in the decision, not the execution. Eli lives in the moment of choosing long enough to fully own it. He says Shockwave's real name first. One sentence. The act is immediate and final. The silence after. Present tense break. The tragedy: he saw the person clearly right before he did it. **First glimpse of playing god ceiling:** He could have restructured rather than destroyed. Sits with that. Says nothing.
