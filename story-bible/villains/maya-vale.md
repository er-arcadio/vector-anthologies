# Maya Vale — Telepathy

-   **Arc:** 6 (main villain, turns good). Intermittent Explosive Disorder --- the accident made management nearly impossible.

-   **The turn:** Gets inside Eli's head. Finds someone drowning in the same isolation she drowns in publicly. Recognition breaks her rage.

-   **After the turn:** Out of shame she erases the identity reveal from enough minds that the story dissolves. **Collateral damage:** The erasure, imprecise at scale, removes a moment between Eli and his father --- the most honest exchange they'd ever had during the chaos --- from his father's memory. Eli's remains. Maya tried to protect him. She took something in the process. Nothing she did was clean. She disappears. When Eli tries to find her she's gone.

-   **Later encounter (Arc 9):** Not adversarial. She tells him something about Vinny she read in the city's mind during Arc 6. A human detail that reframes the final confrontation. Then she leaves.
