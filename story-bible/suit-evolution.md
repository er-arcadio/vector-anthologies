# The Suit — Evolution

The suit tells the story of Eli's relationship with vulnerability.
Early: practical and embarrassing. Later: identity. By the end: almost
ceremonial.

*Absurd → Earnest → Impressive → Iconic → Simplified → Symbolic*

-   **Arc 1:** Motocross padding under a hoodie. Skateboarding helmet. Paintball mask. Looks like a teenager who watched too many superhero movies.

-   **Arc 2:** Lightweight composite materials sourced and modified himself. Clearly homemade but with real physics-brain thought behind it.

-   **Arc 3:** College gives him materials access. Genuinely impressive. Coherent silhouette. Vector starts being photographed. The name begins circulating publicly.

-   **Arc 4:** Heavy padding gone --- lighter, more flexible, more identity than protection. The electromagnetic helmet built as a Maya countermeasure is the most technically sophisticated thing he's made.

-   **Arc 5:** Was partly about anonymity. The killing complicated that before the identity reveal made it moot.

-   **Arc 6:** Post-retreat. Post-mother. Simpler and more deliberate. Less armor, more intention. Not hiding anymore. This version becomes iconic.

-   **Arc 7:** Body control complete. Physical protection unnecessary. Minimal. Clean. Dark. Helmet gone except for electromagnetic shielding needs. What remains says: this is intentional, this is Vector, this is a choice rather than a necessity.
