# Vinny Blackwell → Vinny Black

-   **Age:** One year older than Eli. Oldest in class by default, not failure. One more thing the universe got wrong about him.

-   **Appearance:** White-passing with freckles. Black father (absent, previously abusive), white mother (overwhelmed). Exists between two worlds without fully belonging to either. Medium-length black hair early. Accumulating tattoos across arcs including a cult brand. By Arc 4: more tattoos, black eyeliner, longer thicker hair, black hood. Palette across the arcs trends black with hints of red — the visual opposite number to Vector's white/tan/gold (see `eli-reyes.md` and `../suit-evolution.md`): devil or fallen-angel coded, not superhero-villain coded. Read against his self-healing making him effectively unkillable in a fight, the imagery is deliberate — see `../../writing-guide/story-laws.md` for the Eli/Vinny "tempter" dynamic this feeds.

-   **The Ouroboros ring:** Found on the ground outside the science center on the day of the field trip. No one around. Just there. The omniscient narrator does not explain how it got there. This is intentional. The ring doesn't fit --- he's too young. He keeps it as a necklace. When it later fits his finger he switches to wearing it as a ring. He explains it to a cultist as a life symbol, then quietly says he thinks it means death. The touching gesture --- hand going to the ring when thinking --- is present from Arc 2 onward, unconscious by Arc 7. Its absence, if it ever disappears, is the loudest signal the story can send.

-   **Theo:** Vinny's dog. Named when he was younger and less armored --- a gentle name that slightly embarrasses the teenager he becomes but that he'd never change. Theo's corner in the apartment is the one space Vinny maintains: water bowl clean, blanket folded. Theo's behavior tracks the devil's occupation before any other indicator --- restless in Arc 2/3, staying near the door in Arc 4, not sleeping near Vinny for three weeks before Arc 5. Theo dies quietly in Arc 5. Vinny buries him alone, carefully, smooths the dirt after. Goes inside. No mourning scene. The horror is in the absence of it.

-   **Father:** Absent and previously abusive. The abuse is never shown directly --- only its residue. The flinch when a man raises his voice. The fact that he never sits with his back to a door. The shirt he kept that doesn't fit anymore. The way he confuses someone finally seeing him with someone finally loving him.

-   **What Vinny wants:** Power to be free. Recognition to feel special. He thought he was special when he first discovered his powers --- Vector crushed that. He sees Eli as a brother competing for a father's attention neither of them actually has. Beating Vector is his destiny, his legacy, his dragon to slay. The competitive drive is manic and sincere.

-   **Power --- the reframe:** Vinny's self-healing is not a passive defensive ability. It is an integration mechanism --- his body absorbs damage, foreign power, dark influence, and optimizes against each. By Arc 7 the accumulation of what he has integrated makes him structurally the most powerful being in the story. His final choice is not retreat from defeat. It is retreat from victory. The cost of finishing it would have been the last piece of himself.

-   **Psychological register:** Dexter baseline, not Joker. His empathy starts genuinely high and declines through specific losses and specific choices. The decline is trackable --- the reader can point to the exact moments the needle moved. His intelligence never degrades. What degrades is the weight he gives to other people's experience of his actions. One Joker crack: a laugh that arrives in wrong moments, sounds almost right and then doesn't.

-   **One consistent ordinary thing:** The hand going to the Ouroboros ring when thinking. Present from Arc 2. Unconscious by Arc 7. The persistence of this gesture across everything he loses is the story's most efficient humanity marker.

-   **Arc:** Finds magic book → joins cult (Arc 1) → cult massacre (Arc 3) → first appearance vs Eli, loses (Arc 2) → darker, retaliatory (Arc 3) → short-term time travel, chaos magic, stone destroyed (Arc 5) → devil's influence accelerates (Arc 6) → final boss (Arc 7).

-   **The devil's influence:** Never face-to-face. Through ritual, symbolism, the book. Shadow wrong. Clocks stopping. Animals avoiding him. Theo tracking the change before anyone else. The horror is that there is no single moment the door opened. There is no moment. That's the point.

-   **Arc 7 truth:** Vinny disappearing mid-fight is an act of will. He felt himself becoming a door rather than a person and made his last autonomous choice: close the door. He chose himself at the last possible moment. What that cost him is left unanswered.
