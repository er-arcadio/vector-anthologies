# The Physics Teacher (Mentor)

-   **Profile:** Late 50s, weathered, sharp, unhurried. PhD in physics. Chose to teach high school. Gay, white. Two people who understand in different ways what it means to exist in spaces not originally built for them.

-   **Connection to the accident:** He is the researcher who propped the door open --- one link in the butterfly effect chain. He didn't connect it to Eli's powers. But underneath the teaching there has been a question he couldn't answer: what did that Tuesday cost? His choice to teach high school is what he did with that question. He went where he thought he could matter most to a specific kind of person at a specific moment. He was waiting for Eli without knowing it.

-   **Arc 6 revelation and conversation:** Eli finds his name in the paper trail. Brings it to him sideways. The teacher doesn't deny it. Says something short and true. The conversation is devastating and leaves the relationship intact --- deepened rather than broken. His negligence was human and passive. What he built from it was real.

-   **Core philosophy:** "Nothing is perfect." The direct counterweight to Eli's father. Their conversations are the most important ones that never say what they're actually about.
