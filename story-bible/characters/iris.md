# Iris

-   **Group 1:** Was not in the room. No powers. One scheduling decision away from everything.

-   **Profile:** Quietly perceptive, private. Raised by a steady single father. Notices everything but rarely volunteers what she sees.

-   **What Iris knew and when:** She recognized Eli's name from the Group 1 list years before Colombia --- had been watching Vector's story from the outside. When they met at the retreat she already knew who she was talking to. Said nothing as the relationship developed from hookup to friendship to something real. When the field trip revelation comes she tells him this separately --- its own beat, after the shared origin discovery lands. He cannot be angry. The irony is exact: he spent his entire life hiding his identity from everyone he's ever cared about, and the first person he was truly honest with was honest with him slightly less than completely. What it gives him is more important than what it costs: someone who saw him, knew what they were seeing, and stayed anyway. Knowing and staying. Until Iris this had only ever been true of his mother.

-   **Colombia:** The retreat is Iris's established sanctuary --- she has been several times, it is her known getaway. The identity exposure sent her there naturally. Eli arrives independently, by coincidence --- he ran and ended up somewhere with a bed and no signal. Their shared wound brought them to the same place without either of them knowing. The relationship begins as a hookup --- relief, meaningless. He also hooks up with a Colombian local; she may witness this. Drift into friendship. Trust builds slowly. Second hookup carries more meaning. Late night conversation reveals shared Jersey City origin, same field trip, opposite rooms. Then: she tells him she recognized his name before they met.

-   **Role:** The first person he cannot perform for. His mother was safety. Iris is challenge. She doesn't ask him to be Vector. She asks him to be Eli. After his mother dies she becomes the moral anchor --- more difficult and more transformative for being challenge rather than comfort.

-   **The time conversation (Arc 7):** In a casual conversation she describes how she perceives time. The door it opens in his understanding of what he's always been doing when he freezes motion is the last piece of his Arc 7 breakthrough. Not earned through study. Through intimacy. Through actually listening.
