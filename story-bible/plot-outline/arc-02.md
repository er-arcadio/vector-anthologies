# Arc 2 — Age 17.5, Ending at 18th Birthday

-   Shockwave's backstory unfolds --- audience knows who he was before seeing what he's become.

-   Eli uncovers Shockwave's identity after first fight. Contained at arc's end.

-   **Vinny Black first appearance --- dual POV:** Dressed in black, medium hair, tattoos, cult brand, glowing stone. Eli has the advantage the whole time. Vinny gives up and disappears through magic. From Eli's POV: strange kid, outmatched, ran. Filed under: possibly dangerous, not a priority. From omniscient narrator: Vinny goes home. Sits in the dark. Theo puts his head on Vinny's knee. Vinny puts his hand on Theo's head and sits there for a long time. Not crying. Just sitting. The gap isn't talent --- it's preparation. Those are different problems with different solutions. He opens the book to a page he hasn't opened before. Inside the cult the rank-and-file fear him --- they can feel he's channeling something they can't name and misread that fear as jealousy. One cult member who disappeared is mentioned once in passing. No explanation. Nobody asks.

-   Reporter byline appears for the first time. A name. Nothing more.

-   18th birthday as arc's closing beat --- earned rather than given.
 *The horror this arc lives in Vinny's first appearance and disappearance. The omniscient narrator shows the other side --- Vinny going home and sitting in the dark for a long time. Not crying. Just sitting. Write that scene in the same flat prose as Arc 1. The horror is the stillness of it. The second horror beat is the cult rank-and-file: they are afraid of him because they can feel he is channeling something they cannot name. One member who disappeared should be mentioned once in passing and never again. No explanation. Nobody asks. The reader will ask. That is enough.*
