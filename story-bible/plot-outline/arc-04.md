# Arc 4 — College, Age 22

-   Twitch first full appearance: Flash-level capability, time dilation effects. Twitch captured. Foreshadowing Vinny's time magic complete.

-   **Sol (Marisol, Latina):** Quick-witted, sees through his personas. Reputation preceded her --- approaches with caution as a game, becomes a sucker for his genuine charm. He ends it when he starts to feel something real. "You're great but it's not the right time for us." She leaves saying one true thing he carries for years.

-   Simone (Shifter) introduced under Maya's mind control. Arc ends without threat resolved --- first time.

-   **Vinny --- omniscient narrator:** One scene. A new space suggesting something built in the silence. Theo stays near the exit. Vinny doesn't notice. The book open to a page that seems to rearrange when the light changes. He doesn't notice that either. One sentence: Theo hasn't slept near him in three weeks.
 *No major horror spike this arc. The hum continues in Vinny's absence --- which is itself the horror register. He doesn't appear and the reader starts to wonder what he is becoming in the silence. If Vinny appears briefly, write him with one detail that is slightly harder to place --- a laugh that sounds almost right, a reference to something from before that lands slightly wrong. Keep it brief. Keep it flat. The reader's shoulders should go up slightly and then the scene should end.*
