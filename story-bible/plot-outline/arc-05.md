# Arc 5 — Graduation, Age 22

-   Opens behind: Simone still winning. Early resolution --- novel matter to contain her. Simone freed, disoriented, horrified.

-   **Theo's death:** Before the stone is destroyed. Morning. Two sentences. Vinny buries him alone. Carefully. Smooths the dirt after. Goes inside. No mourning scene. The horror is in the absence of it.

-   **Last recognizable moment:** After the burial, before the fight. Vinny in a diner. Midday. Quiet. He orders something specific. Watches a table of kids his age laughing. The person he could have been is fully visible in this scene. Not stated. Just present in the watching. His shadow moves wrong. He doesn't notice. We do. Then he pays. He leaves.

-   Cory dies in his cell: Vinny extracts his strength. Transfer kills Cory. Power goes to Shockwave. Yellow Hulk-like transformation.

-   **The Killing:** Shockwave transformed, in constant pain, involuntary discharges, civilian casualties mounting, every containment attempt failing. The not-instantaneous is in the decision. Eli lives in the moment of choosing long enough to fully own it. He says Shockwave's real name. One sentence. The act is immediate and final. Silence after. Present tense break. Two tracks that must never bleed into each other: visceral necessity building on one, Eli saying the real name on the other. The tragedy: he saw the person clearly right before he did it. The guilt has a specific shape: the knowledge of what he could have done instead came one fight too late.

-   Earthquake rescue (Arc 5 or early 6): no villain, just consequence and precision. Tonal contrast to the killing. The Arc 1 earthquake on TV connects here on reread.

-   Vinny second appearance: stone destroyed. Theo gone. Cult gone. Cult leader gone. He opens the book. The page it opens to he's never seen before.

-   Aftermath: killing becomes public. Father delivers verdict at dinner not knowing. Physics teacher's oblique conversation. Surgical residency begins.
 *Two horror spikes this arc, different registers. The first is the Shockwave killing --- thrilling tragedy, not pure horror. Write it on two simultaneous tracks that must never bleed into each other. Track one: visceral and necessary --- the yellow transformation, involuntary discharges, civilian casualties mounting, every containment attempt failing. The reader feels the necessity building. Track two: Eli saying Shockwave's real name before the end. One sentence. His real name. Then the act. Then silence. The tragedy is not that Eli killed someone --- it is that he saw the person clearly right before he did it. The thrill and the grief arrive simultaneously and the reader cannot choose between them. The second horror beat is Vinny's last recognizable moment --- write it before the stone is destroyed. The person he could have been fully visible. Then his shadow moves wrong. He does not notice. We do. This is the last time the reader will see him clearly. Write it with that weight without announcing it.*
