# Opening

A glimpse: Eli alone in a ruined city block, looking at his hands. City
quiet. Retrospective voice: "I knew his name. I'd known it since the
first time we fought. I want you to know that before I tell you anything
else." Then: his 16th birthday. Cake. Family tension. The contrast
between those two images is the entire story in two pages.
