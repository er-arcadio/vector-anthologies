# Arc 6 — Age 23-26

-   Surgical residency: technically brilliant, emotionally absent. Patients stop being problems and start being people. Selflessness through practice not revelation.

-   Apathy deepens. What is all of this for?

-   Maya Vale: city-wide chaos, mind-controls civilians including Simone as first proxy. Eli comes out in electromagnetic helmet --- pushing people away without hurting them. Almost absurd, genuinely difficult.

-   Extended confrontation: Maya gets inside Eli's head. Finds someone drowning in the same isolation she drowns in publicly. Recognition breaks her rage.

-   Identity reveal: Maya exposes Eli. Mother killed by random civilian who saw the news --- not mind-controlled, not connected to any plan. Most mundane and therefore most devastating loss. Write it without transition. She is alive and then she is not and the prose does not pause.

-   Maya's erasure: out of shame she erases the identity reveal. Collateral damage --- a moment of honesty between Eli and his father during the chaos is erased from his father's memory. Eli's remains. Maya disappears. Eli figures out what she did.

-   Grief: father and son, two men who loved the same woman sitting in the space she left. The father admits he suspected. Not that he knew --- that he wondered and chose not to look too closely. Avoidance dressed as standards.

-   The playing god ceiling fully confronted: could rewrite the accident's effects at a cellular level. Won't. The refusal is active and daily.

-   **Origin investigation:** Butterfly effect chain. Physics teacher's name in the paper trail. Their subsequent conversation. Danny Reeves and bio-control kid. The Group 1 list --- Iris's name, the reporter's name. National reckoning.

-   Eli runs. Colombia. Four to six months. Meditation as the retreat's bread-and-butter --- he starts going because Iris is going and he's attracted to her. Slowly understands its significance. The sessions surface what he's been filing away. Pain before relief.

-   **Iris:** Hookup begins as pure relief. Colombian local hookup too. Drift into friendship. Something true escapes before he can file it away. She receives it without making it a moment. Second hookup carries more meaning. Late night conversation: shared Jersey City origin, same field trip, opposite rooms. Then: she tells him she recognized his name before they met. He cannot be angry. The irony is exact.

-   Joel Mara (Sound Manipulator): persuaded barely. Reappears on the right side in the final arc.

-   Meteor (Arc 6 or early 7): first time in space by choice. Seeds moon fight.

-   Voss: field perception across entire city. Every device found by electrochemical signature. Killed simultaneously. Not a physical fight. A man in a room who says something that sounds like Eli's father reframed as warning.
 *Three horror spikes this arc, each a different register. First: Maya --- psychological horror. The horror is not the mind control. It is the accuracy. When she gets inside Eli's head she does not find a hero. She finds someone drowning in the same water she has been drowning in for years. Write his interior in this moment as the reader has always known it --- then make it visible to someone inside the story. The violation is the exposure. Her disappearance afterward --- no resolution, no confrontation, just gone --- is the horror's final note. Do not resolve it. Second: Eli's mother's death --- mundane horror, the most important horror beat in the story. No villain. No plan. No meaning. A civilian who saw the news. Write it without transition. She is alive and then she is not and the prose does not pause to mark the difference. The horror is the refusal of narrative logic. Resist every instinct to make this moment dramatic. Its power is entirely in its plainness. Third: the playing god ceiling --- Eli's mirror fear. Write this realization quietly --- not as revelation but as arrival. The horror is not the capability. It is that refusing it is a choice he will have to remake every day for the rest of his life. And that the choice might get slightly easier to not make. And that he might not notice until it already has not been made.*
