# Arc 3 — College, Age 19-21

-   Physical confidence and style emerging. Working out --- visibly more muscular by mid-arc. Making friends, having fun, speaking louder. Irish goodbye personality established.

-   Physics classes directly expanding capability. Knowledge-dependency most visible here.

-   **Jade (Korean-American):** First hookup, college party. Eli misreads the energy --- one moment of genuine connection, she laughs and he holds it too long. Sees her with someone else at a later party. Grabs another girl out of spite. Builds the first brick of the wall without knowing it.

-   Fuckboy phase begins. Personas constructed. Isolation accentuating underneath the performance.

-   Tide (Marcus Webb): not evil --- cornered by registration pressure. Major public incident. Tide's tsunami: two simultaneous problems, scale demonstration, must be written to full dramatic potential. Tide captured at arc's end.

-   Citywide blackout from electricity attempt --- civilian consequences Eli initially treats as technical problem.

-   Twitch: brief teaser only. Seeds Arc 4.

-   **Vinny --- omniscient narrator:** The cult leader's blood transfer attempt kills him --- his body cannot integrate Vinny's cellular byproducts. Vinny is left alone inside a group that never liked him. His mother says something careless and true that lands as a verdict on his entire existence. The camera stays on the outside of the door after he closes it. He breaks from the cult, pursues forbidden techniques, then returns for inventory. The massacre happens in a remote closed hiking trail --- only a few birds disturbed, no one heard a thing. The cult was populated by people existing at the margins. One member has someone who marks their existence twice a year. That person eventually notices the silence and files a report. The system is slow. Vinny walks home. Theo is at the door. He sits on the floor. Theo climbs into his lap --- too big. He lets it. One sentence about his hands. Then nothing. First time the shadow moves wrong. One sentence. Vinny does not notice. We do. Theo looks at the corner where the shadow is wrong. Settles.
 *This arc is where the hum becomes permanent. The cult leader's death --- write it quietly, not dramatically. The horror is in what follows: Vinny alone inside a group that never liked him, the warmth gone. His mother says something careless and true and the omniscient narrator stays on the outside of the door after he closes it. That image is the arc's horror thesis. From this point forward something is different about Vinny's scenes. The shadow moves wrong once. Theo looks at a corner of the room. Write it in the same flat prose. Do not linger. The horror is that it is already becoming normal.*
