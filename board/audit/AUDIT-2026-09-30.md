## The headline

The problem is not that there isn't enough here. It is that the project is **one level of detail short of drafting**, and a few load-bearing canon facts contradict each other. The bible is deep and well organised, the writing guide is genuinely rigorous, and the site works. What is missing is the layer between "this arc's premise" and "this story's want, obstacle and A-to-B" — plus agreement on four or five facts that several files currently answer differently.

## 2. The blockers that actually matter

**B1. The Maya-erasure cluster contradicts itself.** Five files say the honest father-son moment survives only on Eli's side; `era-1-overview.md` says the opposite, twice. The phrase "starts over from behind" only works one of those ways. Arcs 6 and 7 cannot be drafted coherently until this is one ledger. → **T-005**

**B2. Event order is unsettled.** Whether the father's admission sits in Arc 6 or Arc 7; whether Eli learns before Colombia that the erasure held; who retains the identity reveal; Voss appearing in both Arc 6 and Arc 7; the Maya intel drop placed in both Arc 7 and Arc 9. → **T-006**

**B3. Names that block drafting do not exist.** Shockwave has no real name anywhere in canon, and two slate stories hinge on Eli saying it aloud. Eli's mother, father and the physics teacher are unnamed, and the father has no job, faith or interior — which every Arc 1 story needs. → **T-009, T-010**

**B4. A hard story law is broken in the outline.** `era-1-overview.md` says Eli cannot move a mind "categorically, not yet", against `story-laws.md` treating that as a categorical boundary rather than a gap. → **T-008**

**B5. The Iris reveal rests on a premise canon contradicts.** `arc-00.md` puts Eli and Iris in the same class, yet the shared-origin discovery is staged as news; and her "years before Colombia" recognition has no mechanism. → **T-007**

## 3. Where the pipeline breaks

The documented path is worksheet → slate → outline checklist → style guide and laws → draft → eval → board. It breaks at the **slate-to-outline step**:

- `era-1-overview.md` gives main character, problem, obstacles and resolution. The required story-shape block and `eval-checklist.md` need **want, need, Point A, Point B, obstacle type, cost and `story_type`**. Those do not exist for any story.
- The outline step has no template, no home, and no approval state. There is no way to record that an outline was agreed.
- Nothing says which story to write next. The overview calls itself "a map, not a queue", and an older "Recommended Writing Order" contradicts it.
- The eval was run by the drafter on the only existing draft, and missed four canon errors the board later caught.
- There is no defined path for a flagged story: no revise, no retire.

## 4. What is public that you may not intend to be

The repo is public and the reading site is public. `build_site.py` filters out only `CLAUDE.md` and `*-review.md` — **it does not filter on `canon_status` or `eval_status`**, so every story file under `stories/` publishes automatically. `the-name-first.md` is therefore live on the reading site right now despite being `canon_status: draft` and `eval_status: flagged`.

This also means the board files added by this system are readable on GitHub regardless of the encrypted site tab. → **T-013, T-012, T-015**

## 5. Backlog drift

Four backlogs, not two: `TODO.md`, `story-bible/action-items-v5.1.md`, `story-bible/open-questions.md`, and the overview's own "Open Gaps". They disagree: the names that block Arc 1 are last priority in action-items but early in open-questions' writing order; `open-questions.md` treats Vinny's fate and the devil's endgame as live decisions while `story-laws.md` says they are deliberately closed; and the hero's-journey overlay sitting in `TODO.md` is a narrative task, not tooling. Neither queue lists "Eighteen", the paper-trail mechanism, Brick's origin, the suit colour transitions, or the erasure ledger.

Documentation drift worth a single cleanup sitting: a 1,500-4,000 word target in `writing-guide/CLAUDE.md` against 3,000-5,000 in the style guide; "Currently empty" in `stories/CLAUDE.md`; a phantom "Section 0" reference in `outline-checklist.md`.
