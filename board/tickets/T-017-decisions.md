## 3 ★ When Vinny vanished, did he go there too — or did he only send Eli?

**My fault — I explained this badly.** The word was "alone", not "along": `era-1-overview.md` says *"Eli is immediately flung **alone** onto an alien planet."* I then quoted it without saying what the actual question was.

Here it is plainly.

**Canon, today:** at the end of the Arc 7 fight, Vinny closes the door and vanishes. Where he goes is never said. Separately, his magic drops Eli on the strange planet. Eli is **by himself** there, and Arc 8 is built on that solitude.

**Your thought:** at the end of Era 1, *Vinny* went back into that world.

**So the question is just:** are they both in that world after the fight, or only Eli?

| | What happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Vinny **only sends** Eli there. Vinny himself goes wherever he goes — still unanswered, as canon has it. His years in that world explain why his magic reached for it. | **Nothing.** Arc 8 and Arc 9 stand exactly as written. |
| **(b)** | Vinny went there too, but **earlier or briefly** — gone before Eli arrives. Eli finds traces: practice grounds, supers who remember a pale boy who came and went for years. | Small. Arc 8 keeps its isolation and gains dread. |
| **(c)** | Both there **at the same time**, Eli never knowing. | Large. Arc 8's isolation becomes a lie the reader is in on. |
| **(d)** | Both there and they **meet**. | Very large. Rewrites Era 2, and Arc 9's *"Vinny's legacy"* becomes wrong — he'd be a presence, not a residue. |

**Recommended: (a).** It is the one that actually matches what you said in decision 2 — keep it as it was, add the why — and it is quietly the most brutal reading available:

> Vinny, feeling himself stop being a person, reaches in his last conscious moment for the one place that was ever his. And what the reach does is send **Eli** there instead. He gives away his refuge without meaning to, and never knows he did.

That is the foreshadowing you were after, it costs nothing, and it leaves (b) available later if you ever want the planet haunted.

## 4 Raunchy as texture — how far does this go?

You described the register as **"descriptively raunchy (both smutty with sex and bloody with battle)."**

Canon permits this but subordinates it. `style-guide.md`: *"Adult content, including sex scenes, is permitted **where it serves character arc — never as decoration**"* and *"**30% Visceral Action** — violence that shocks because it's **earned, not decorative**."*

You framed the raunchiness under *"what makes it different / unique / better"* — i.e. as a reason to read the thing. That's a slightly different claim from "it earns its place through arc."

(a) Keep the existing rule — explicitness is permitted but must still serve arc
(b) Loosen it — tonal texture is itself a legitimate reason, and the guide's "never as decoration" softens to "never gratuitous"
(c) Split it — the visceral register is a *world* property (allowed to exist for atmosphere), while anything involving a named character still has to serve their arc

**Recommended: (c).** It gives you the texture you're describing without licensing decorative sex or violence involving people the reader cares about.
