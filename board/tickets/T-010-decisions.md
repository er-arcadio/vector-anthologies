# T-010 — the 13 decisions

**How to use this file.** One decision per block. Comment on any line in the PR's *Files changed* tab to push back, ask, or choose something other than the recommendation. Fill in **Author's call** inline if you prefer — either works, I read both.

**★ = load-bearing.** These five change what gets written. The other eight are taste and can be deferred without blocking anything.

Reasoning, evidence and the filled worksheets are in `T-010-proposal.md`. You should not need it to decide — it is there if a recommendation looks wrong and you want to see why it was made.

## 1 ★ Family heritage

(a) Puerto Rican father / Dominican mother · (b) both Puerto Rican · (c) both Dominican · (d) Afro-Panamanian / Garifuna · (e) Colombian

**Recommended: (a).** Reyes works for both, explains the grandfather's shop timeline, puts two registers in one kitchen for free.

**Avoid (e)** — it makes Arc 6 Colombia stop being a coincidence, which the Iris reveal is built on.

**Cost:** whichever you pick, the birthday scene in `the-name-first.md` changes. That draft is flagged and already slated for rewrite, so the cost is likely nil — but it is your call, and it couples this to T-012.
